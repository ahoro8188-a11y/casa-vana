Our Story

A family coffee estate, opened up as a home for travellers.

Casa Vana sits on a slope of the same coffee estate our family has tended for three generations, an hour outside Madikeri. When the old plantation bungalow needed new life, we rebuilt it slowly — keeping the wide verandah, the wood-fired kitchen, and the silence.

Today it holds three bedrooms, one kitchen the whole house gathers around, and a view of the Western Ghats that changes every hour. We host one group at a time — the whole property is yours, not shared with strangers down the hall.
